# Art Direction - Awwwards-Level Digital Craft

Guidelines for elevating digital products to international design award recognition (Awwwards Site of the Day / FWA / CSSDA).

## 1. Editorial Layout & Asymmetry
- **Beyond the Grid:** Break out of uniform card matrices. Use asymmetric proportions (60/40, 70/30), overlapping typography over imagery, negative space as an active compositional element, and varying scale.
- **Visual Tension & Balance:** Counterbalance large organic photo frames with disciplined typographic captions and refined hairline delimiters (`rgba(20, 34, 54, 0.08)`).
- **Curated Vignettes:** Treat each section as an editorial magazine spread.

## 2. Emotional Metaphors (Acoustic Resonance & Precision)
- **Visualizing Sound:** For OTUS Soluções Auditivas, the core experience is regaining acoustic intimacy with life.
- Use interactive or animated acoustic waveforms (`canvas` or animated SVG paths), subtle ripple pulses that evoke sound waves propagating smoothly through space, and sound spectrum indicators.
- Evoke physical materials: brushed aluminum, warm medical ceramic, acoustic mesh textures.

## 3. High-End Micro-Details
- **Interactive Cursor / Magnetic Accents:** Subtle magnetic hover attraction on key CTAs and interactive cards.
- **Glass & Atmosphere:** Multi-layered radial ambient lighting (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` with subtle tinting), never harsh flat shapes.
- **Custom Browser Polish:** Custom highlighted text (`::selection`), smooth momentum scrolling, refined scroll indicator, fluid interactive audio test or hearing simulator widget.

## 4. Typography Mastery
- Juxtapose high-contrast editorial serifs (Playfair Display / Cormorant) for expressive emotional headlines with utilitarian, ultra-precise sans-serifs (Outfit / Inter) for clinical facts, credentials, and steps.
- Use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for statistics, phone numbers, and time intervals.
- Maintain impeccable vertical cadence and baseline alignment.
