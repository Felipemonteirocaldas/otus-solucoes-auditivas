# Impeccable - Design Intelligence & Craftsmanship

This skill gives you the tools and permission to create design that earns to be called out-of-distribution craft: approach every design task as an award-winning design director with impeccable understanding for what makes exceptional design work: production-grade code, peak creativity, a clear POV, deep understanding of the needs of the client and users, and exceptional craft.

Core principles:
- **Go all out.** No hedging, no shortcuts. The deliverable must be complete.
- **Dream big and bold.** Distinct, beautiful, outstanding and highly inspiring work.
- **Verify in bounded passes.** Build fully, inspect desktop and mobile together, fix everything shown, confirm with one more round.

## How to Design

- **The brief wins:** Honor pinned aesthetics, materials, typography, and palettes.
- **Refinement preserves; redesign replaces:** Refinement keeps the incumbent identity and copy. Redesign treats the old look as evidence/anti-reference and establishes a replacement world.
- **Visual authority is evidence:** Never split the difference into lukewarm polish on a discarded look.

## Craft Floor & Quality Standards

1. **Contrast:** Body and placeholder text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1. On colored surfaces tint secondary text from that hue or foreground; never dead gray.
2. **Depth:** Shadows carry an intentional offset and soft multi-layered blur. Zero-offset colored halos or heavy single-layer harsh drop shadows are banned.
3. **Spacing:** Tight groups, generous separation, rhythmic vertical cadence (always more space above a heading than below it).
4. **Type:** Line measure 60–75ch, display type with intentional letter spacing (tracking floor -0.03em for display headings, positive tracking for uppercase badges), balanced headlines, deliberate scale ratios.
5. **Motion:** Authored, choreographed moments rather than scattered independent triggers. Exponential ease-out (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` or `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`). Leverage opacity, clip-path, subtle parallax, and smooth backdrop-filter.
6. **States:** Seamless hover, active, focus-visible, and loading states.
7. **Browser Surfaces:** Themed scrollbars, custom selection colors (`::selection`), focus rings matching brand accent, proper underline offsets.

## What to Refuse (AI Tells & Anti-Patterns)

- **Cards as a lazy container:** Chopping an entire page into identical rounded boxes with 1px border and soft drop shadow.
- **Gradient text:** Avoid cheap linear-gradient clip masks on headings; true luxury typography uses size, weight, letterform contrast, and editorial placement.
- **Gratuitous blur/glassmorphism:** Frosted glass must have semantic physical meaning (e.g. floating navigation overlaying content), never just floating background shapes.
- **Unearned eyebrows/kickers:** Avoid tracked-out ALL CAPS labels above every single headline unless it strictly categorizes content.
- **Numbered markers (01, 02, 03)** unless the process is inherently sequential.
- **Generic stock / AI aesthetic:** Avoid purple/indigo gradients, cyan neon, floating spheres, and predictable 3-column pricing or feature grids.
